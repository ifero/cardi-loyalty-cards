## Intent

**Problem:** The shipped mark, three white bars crossed high by a beam stroke (Story 20.3), reads as unnatural. On 2026-10-30 ifero chose **3C Tessera** to replace it, superseding 20.3's "no card" rule. But every icon, the wordmark's drawn ì and Welcome's brand moment D still draw the old mark.

**Approach:** Define the new mark once and read it from both generators: a cream capsule stem under a beam ID-1 card turned +35°, with its five-bar barcode cut through the card. Regenerate every artefact from it, carry the same card into the wordmark's ì, and redraw brand moment D as "scan, then settle" for the new mark.

**Decisions (ifero, 2026-10-30):**

- **Brand moment D sweeps top to bottom.** The design system's motion rule stands as written: "A scan beam appears full width and sweeps top to bottom". A horizontal beam as wide as the card sweeps the level card downward and reveals it. This replaces the left-to-right prototype.
- **The full spec is kept** at about 3,100 tokens. The intro is not split off, so Welcome never animates the old mark next to the new icon.

## Boundaries & Constraints

**Always:**

- **One definition.** The mark's numbers live in one module, which `build-brand-icons.mjs` and `brand-wordmark.mjs` both read. Values are on the 1024 design grid of `docs/design/cardi/logo-concepts/final/cardi-icon.svg`:
  - stem 156 × 400 at (456, 462), radius 78;
  - card 264 × 166, radius 28, centred on (512, 270), turned **+35°**;
  - bars 26 / 40 / 18 / 32 / 22 wide, 18 apart, radius 3, under a 62 band, inside a 26 margin.

  Positions are relative. The generator centres the mark's box as it does today.

- **Colours.** The icon's stem is cream `#F0F0E8` and its card is beam. The themed and monochrome layers are one colour (white), as today. The wordmark's letters and stem keep their current colours.
- **The bars are holes**, in every transparent raster and every SVG (`fill-rule="evenodd"`). They are never ink paint.
- **Platform rules still hold:**
  - `icon.png` and the other opaque icons are RGB with no alpha; the transparent ones stay RGBA.
  - `cardi-mark.svg` matches `splash-icon.png`.
  - The Android scale is derived and keeps every Wear density under the 66dp logo radius.
  - The watch icons keep their ink inside 0.85 of the circle.
- **Brand moment D:**
  - lasts 0.84 s;
  - shows the stem alone at frame 0;
  - rests on exactly the static mark;
  - uses only the Lottie subset (no masks or mattes);
  - the beam cuts on and never fades;
  - the beam is horizontal, as wide as the card, and sweeps it top to bottom.
- **Tests** that pin the old geometry are updated to the new derived values, never deleted.

**Never:**

- Commit, push, or change `sprint-status.yaml` or any other tracker file. The orchestrator owns all three.
- Hand-edit generated assets.
- Edit `docs/design/cardi/frames/**`, `docs/design/cardi/tools/**`, the logo-concept SVGs, or the records of Stories 20.3 and 20.4.
- Change the Welcome drawing's timing, the launch screen's motion, or `AppIconHeader`'s layout.
- Add dependencies.

## Design Notes

- **Holes.**
  - Raster: card coverage from `max(dCard, −min(dBar))` in the card's rotated frame.
  - SVG: the card is one `<path fill-rule="evenodd">` of rounded-rect subpaths built from quarter arcs, inside `<g transform="rotate(35 cx cy)">`. The card comes first; each bar follows as a subpath.
- **Android scale.** The current formula, 33 ÷ the box-corner radius, gives ×0.846. That puts the stem's foot at about 33.3dp, which fails the 66dp test. Derive the scale instead from the furthest drawn point of the true rounded shapes, against the 66dp logo radius less one **mdpi** pixel, 1dp (about ×0.823). Phone and Wear share it.
  - The margin is set by the phone, not by Wear (amended 2026-10-31). The generator draws Wear's mipmaps itself, but Expo's prebuild resizes `adaptive-icon.png` and `adaptive-icon-monochrome.png` into the phone's mipmaps, and that resizing spreads the edge by most of a pixel. With one hdpi pixel of margin, the mdpi foreground and monochrome reached 33.24dp.
- **Phone guards.** New `test/android-phone-icons.test.ts` runs the resizing Expo's prebuild runs on the phone's assets and measures the result:
  - It calls `generateImageAsync` from `@expo/image-utils` with the arguments `@expo/prebuild-config` passes, with its cache in a temporary directory.
  - It decodes the result with that package's `getJimpImageAsync`. Prebuild's output uses adaptive PNG filters, which `test/png-scanlines.ts` refuses by design.
  - It measures ink as `test/wear-icons.test.ts` does: any non-zero alpha, out to the pixel's far corner.
  - It asserts:
    - **Adaptive layers.** The foreground and the monochrome layer, at mdpi to xxxhdpi (108dp × density, `resizeMode: 'cover'`, transparent), keep their ink within 33dp of the centre.
    - **Splash.** The splash, resized as `expo-splash-screen` does it (`imageWidth` from `app.json` × density, `resizeMode: 'contain'`, centred on a 288dp canvas), keeps its ink within Android 12's 192dp splash-icon circle, a 96dp radius, at every density.
- **The watch containment claim.** The mark's box overshoots the inscribed square by about 0.57 units vertically, at corners where the mark has no ink. Its ink sits within about 0.72 of the circle. State that, rather than the inscribed square, in the generator comment and `cardi-watch-grammar.md:947`.
- **Intro.**
  - The card is drawn whole: beam, with its bars painted ink, because the disc is always ink.
  - An ink cover rect sits over the level card. Its top edge rides the beam down, animating `p` and `s` linearly.
  - The beam is horizontal. It is 18⁄166 of the card's height thick, and as wide as the card plus 34⁄166 of its height on each side.
  - Keyframes, in % of 6 s:
    - on at 2 (hold), at the card's top edge;
    - swept to the bottom edge and off at 8.333;
    - turn from 9 to 12.333, reaching the angle + 3.5;
    - settle at 14, on the angle.
- **Wordmark card.** The icon's card scaled by 12⁄156, centred 22⁄156 stem widths left of the stem's centre, 3 units above the stem's top. The same five bars, scaled.
